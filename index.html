3. Calculation Methodology & Reference Factors

Our model uses standardized Hong Kong carbon emission factors adapted for student daily life:

Activity Category Input Metric Emission Factor Formula Applied
Transport Distance (km) MTR: 0.04 | Bus: 0.08 | Walk: 0 kg/km km × factor
Food Number of Meals Beef: 6.5 | Pork/Chicken: 2.5 | Veg: 1.0 kg/meal meals × factor
Electricity / AC Duration (Hours) 0.80 kg CO2e / hour (HK Grid Avg) hours × 0.80
Consumables Sheets & Bottles Paper: 0.01 kg/sheet | Bottle: 0.15 kg/bottle (sheets × 0.01) + (bottles × 0.15)
